LOGO SETUP INSTRUCTIONS
=======================

To add your Takaful Oman Insurance logo:

1. Place your logo file in this directory with the name: logo.png

2. Supported formats:
   - PNG (recommended)
   - JPG/JPEG
   - SVG

3. Recommended dimensions:
   - Width: 200-300 pixels
   - Height: 60-80 pixels
   - Aspect ratio: approximately 3:1 or 4:1

4. If no logo is found, the application will automatically display:
   "Takaful Oman Insurance SAOG" as text

5. The logo will appear in the top-left of the sidebar navigation

For best results, use a logo with a transparent background (PNG) or one that matches the dark theme.
